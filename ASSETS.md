# Asset credits

## Preview portrait

`demo-photo.jpg` shows **Girl with a Pearl Earring** by **Johannes Vermeer**, circa 1665. The painting and its faithful photographic reproduction are in the public domain.

- [Wikimedia Commons source and public-domain information](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meisje_met_de_parel.jpg)
- [Original image file](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d7/Meisje_met_de_parel.jpg)

The demo renders this artwork as red “I love you” lettering on black, using the photo's brightness to reveal the portrait. Gifts use the photo chosen by their sender.
